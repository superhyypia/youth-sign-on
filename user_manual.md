Rider Guide

What is ClubSpin Register?

ClubSpin Register is the Bray Wheelers ride registration system.

It allows riders to:

* View upcoming spins
* Register for spins
* Share spins with other members
* View route links
* Store emergency contact details
* Quickly re-register for future spins

The system is designed primarily for mobile phones and can also be added to your home screen like an app.

⸻

Viewing Upcoming Spins

Open:

https://braywheelers.ie/clubspins/

You will see all upcoming club spins.

Each spin card shows:

* Spin title
* Date & time
* Meeting point
* Leader
* Distance
* Spin colour / pace
* Route link (if provided)
* Expected weather conditions

⸻

Spin Colours

Colour	Typical Pace
Blue	28 km/h+
Green	26–28 km/h
Yellow	24–26 km/h
Orange	22–24 km/h
Red	20–22 km/h

Choose the spin appropriate to your fitness and experience.

⸻

Registering for a Spin

Tap:

Register

You will be asked for:

* Your name
* Phone number
* Emergency contact name
* Emergency contact phone number
* Optional notes

Once submitted:

* Your registration is saved
* The leader can view your emergency information if required
* Your details are remembered for future spins for 14 days

⸻

Already Registered

If you are already registered:

* The Register button will disappear
* An “Already Registered” message will appear instead

This prevents duplicate registrations.

⸻

Cancelling a Registration

Tap:

Cancel Registration

Then enter:

* Your name
* Phone number

Your registration will be removed.

Please cancel if you cannot attend so leaders know accurate rider numbers.

⸻

Route Links

If a route has been supplied:

Tap:

View Route

This normally opens:

* Strava
* RideWithGPS
* Browser maps

depending on the route supplied.

⸻

Sharing Spins

Tap:

Share Spin

This opens WhatsApp sharing with a direct registration link.

⸻

Emergency Contact Information

ICE = In Case of Emergency.

Your emergency contact details are only visible to:

* Spin leaders
* Club admins

This information is used only if an incident occurs during a spin.

⸻

Rider Best Practices

Before the Spin

* Check the weather indicator
* Bring appropriate clothing
* Ensure your bike is roadworthy
* Carry food and hydration
* Review the route beforehand

⸻

During the Spin

* Follow leader instructions
* Ride predictably
* Respect traffic laws
* Look after newer riders
* Call hazards clearly

⸻

If Plans Change

Always cancel your registration if:

* You are no longer attending
* You are delayed
* You switch to another group

⸻

WhatsApp Spin Groups

After registering:

Keep an eye on the relevant WhatsApp channel for:

* Route changes
* Weather updates
* Delays
* Cancellations

⸻

Progressive Web App (PWA)

You can install ClubSpin Register to your phone.

iPhone

1. Open ClubSpin Register in Safari
2. Tap Share
3. Tap “Add to Home Screen”

Android

1. Open ClubSpin Register in Chrome
2. Tap menu
3. Tap “Install App” or “Add to Home Screen”

Spin Leader Guide

Leader Responsibilities

Spin leaders are responsible for:

* Creating spins
* Managing registrations
* Leading the group safely
* Communicating changes
* Handling emergencies if required

⸻

Logging In

Open:

https://braywheelers.ie/clubspins/admin/

Enter:

* Email
* Password

Leaders remain logged in for approximately 14 days.

⸻

Creating a Spin

From the dashboard:

Tap:

Create New Spin

Required fields:

* Spin title
* Date
* Start time
* Spin colour

Optional fields:

* Distance
* Description
* Strava route
* Rider limit

⸻

Spin Statuses

Draft

* Hidden from riders
* Used while preparing a spin

Final

* Visible publicly
* Riders can register

Cancelled

* Remains visible
* Registration closes

⸻

Using Ride Templates

Leaders can save common rides as templates.

Templates help quickly recreate:

* Saturday spins
* Club league spins
* Standard coffee spins
* Recurring routes

Templates save:

* Title
* Route
* Pace
* Distance
* Description

⸻

Viewing Riders

Tap:

View Riders

Leaders can see:

* Rider names
* Phone numbers
* Emergency contacts
* Notes

This information is intended only for safety management.

⸻

Emergency Situations

If an incident occurs:

1. Ensure the group is safe
2. Contact emergency services if required
3. Use the rider list to access ICE contacts
4. Notify club officials if appropriate

⸻

Leader Best Practices

Before the Spin

* Publish spins early
* Include route links
* Monitor weather
* Communicate changes early
* Keep rider limits realistic

⸻

During the Spin

* Maintain appropriate pace
* Regroup regularly
* Look after newer riders
* Ensure nobody is left behind unnecessarily

⸻

If Cancelling

Update the spin status to:

Cancelled

This automatically closes registration.

Also notify riders through:

* WhatsApp
* Club channels

⸻

Admin Users

Admins have additional permissions:

* Manage users
* Delete spins
* Access Danger Zone
* Manage platform settings

Regular leaders do not see admin-only features.

⸻

Data & Privacy

The platform stores:

* Rider registration data
* Emergency contacts
* Leader accounts

This information is used only for:

* Ride management
* Emergency response
* Club administration

⸻

Troubleshooting

I cannot register

Possible reasons:

* Spin is cancelled
* Spin is full
* You are already registered
